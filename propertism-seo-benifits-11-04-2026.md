FEATURE: DYNAMIC LANDING PAGES (SEO-DRIVEN LEAD ENTRY)

We have introduced a new capability in Propertism where the platform can automatically generate multiple property-specific pages such as:

* Flats for Sale in Chennai
* Villas in Chennai
* Budget Homes under 50 Lakhs

Example live URLs:

* http://propertism.in/chennai/flats-for-sale/
* http://propertism.in/chennai/villas-for-sale/
* http://propertism.in/chennai/flats-under-50-lakhs/

These pages are not manually created. They are dynamically built using the existing property data from the system.

---

WHAT THIS FEATURE DOES

Instead of relying only on a single homepage or generic property listing page, the platform now creates multiple focused pages, each targeting a specific user need or search intent.

Each page:

* Shows relevant properties from the backend
* Contains location-specific content
* Includes direct contact options (WhatsApp / expert connect)

---

HOW USERS REACH THESE PAGES

These pages are designed primarily for discovery:

1. When users search on Google (e.g., “flats for sale in Chennai”), they can land directly on pages like:
   http://propertism.in/chennai/flats-for-sale/

2. Users can also reach them through links within the website (related searches, navigation).

3. These pages can be shared directly via WhatsApp or used in marketing campaigns.

---

BUSINESS IMPACT

This feature transforms Propertism from a static listing site into a lead-generation platform.

Benefits:

* Increases visibility on Google for multiple search terms
* Brings highly relevant users directly to matching property pages
* Improves chances of conversion through focused content and CTAs
* Enables scalable growth without creating pages manually

---

KEY ADVANTAGE

One property database → multiple entry points for users

This means:

* No duplication of effort
* No manual page creation
* Continuous expansion of reach as more properties and cities are added

---

SUMMARY

This feature allows Propertism to capture user intent at the search level and convert it into direct inquiries, making it a more effective and scalable real estate lead platform.
