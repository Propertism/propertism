Phase 1 Summary

By the completion of M2.1–M2.16, the Propertism integration should provide:

Customer-facing capabilities: ~70+ functional features spanning conversational assistance, inquiries, navigation, and guidance.
Administrator capabilities: ~30+ operational and governance features for configuration, knowledge management, security, analytics, and reporting.
Integration capabilities: A complete integration of the existing realBOT Core Platform into Propertism.in, with deterministic business logic, governed knowledge, operational observability, and business intelligence—ready for M2.17 (Production Rollout, Acceptance & Go-Live).
