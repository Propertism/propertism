Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026  ·  Propertism Realty Advisors LLP

Back to Home

At Propertism, your privacy is our highest priority. This policy outlines how we collect, protect, and use your personal information when you interact with our platform and advisory services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information when you register for NRI Assist, sign in via Google, submit a property inquiry, or interact with our property concierge services. This includes name, email address, phone number, country of residence, and property preferences. We do not collect sensitive financial data directly.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide personalized real estate guidance, coordinate with verified property care partners, and enhance your experience on our platform. We may send advisory updates and property alerts if you have opted in to our newsletter.

3. Data Security

All data transmitted through our platform is encrypted using industry-standard TLS protocols and stored in secure cloud environments (AWS). We implement high-grade security measures to protect your portfolio information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is only shared with verified partners — such as legal counsel, property managers, or maintenance coordinators — with your explicit consent and solely for the purpose of service fulfillment.

5. Your Rights

You may request a copy of the data we hold, ask us to correct inaccuracies, or request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us through our Contact section.
