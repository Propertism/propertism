3. Verification of Knowledge Hub "Insights" Breadcrumb

Insights Trail Architecture:
Crumb 1: Home → https://www.propertism.in/
Crumb 2: Insights → https://www.propertism.in/#blog-section
Crumb 3: [Article Title] → https://www.propertism.in/blog/<slug>/

Architecture & Rationale:

  • In Propertism, all blog and knowledge hub articles are presented within the dedicated Insights section on the homepage (/#blog-section).
  • The standalone /blog/ route is configured to redirect to /#blog-section (see content/site_context.py and content/views.py).
  • Setting the "Insights" breadcrumb destination directly to https://www.propertism.in/#blog-section aligns with the site's single-page hub architecture and avoids passing users or search engines through an unnecessary redirect.
  • Decision: Left unchanged as intentional, canonical, and architecturally aligned.
