3. Verification of Knowledge Hub "Insights" Breadcrumb

Insights Trail Architecture:
Crumb 1: Home → https://www.propertism.in/  |  Crumb 2: Insights → https://www.propertism.in/#blog-section  |  Crumb 3: [Article Title] → https://www.propertism.in/blog/<slug>/

Architecture & Rationale: All blog and knowledge hub articles are presented within the dedicated Insights section on the homepage (/#blog-section). The standalone /blog/ route redirects to /#blog-section. Setting the "Insights" breadcrumb destination directly to /#blog-section aligns with the single-page hub architecture and avoids passing crawlers through redirects. Decision: Left unchanged as intentional, canonical, and architecturally aligned.
