Biography

Viji Munuswamy is the Technology Consultant & Systems Architect at Propertism. With over 20 years of technology leadership and 60+ enterprise platform rollouts, he leads the platform architecture and technology integration vision. Viji specializes in building governed, AI-orchestrated platforms at scale, bridging the gap between cutting-edge AI systems and robust, enterprise-grade production environments.

Role within Propertism

As Technology Partner, Viji defines Propertism's long-term technical architecture and product roadmap. He oversees system security, infrastructure stability, and the integration of automated workflows that empower NRI property owners to manage their investments in Chennai with complete visibility and real-time coordination.

Technology Leadership

Pioneered enterprise-level change management and delivery frameworks. Championed the transition from traditional, monolithic application architecture to modular, metadata-driven architectures that scale seamlessly. Led multi-disciplinary technology teams in designing software platforms for Retail, POS, Microfinance, and CRM sectors.

Enterprise Platform Experience

Successfully delivered 60+ enterprise platform rollouts globally over a span of two decades. Specialized in designing cohesive, high-performance backends using Python, Django, PostgreSQL, and REST APIs, while managing complex frontend integrations in Next.js and React. Built robust financial, customer relationship, and operational workflows for high-compliance environments.

AI & Innovation

Architecting next-generation multi-agent systems and governed AI workflows. Leads the integration of generative AI layers into core operational systems, utilizing Gemini, Claude, and OpenAI within structured frameworks. Prototyped and built the realBOT roadmaps for autonomous property workflows, ensuring all AI applications adhere to strict governance and human-in-the-loop validation.

Technology Vision

"Technology should eliminate time-zone and geographic barriers. Our vision is to build an autonomous, highly transparent property management ecosystem. By combining governed AI orchestration (realBOT) with secure, real-time data pipelines, we give NRI owners the absolute confidence that their assets are managed, protected, and optimized as if they were on the ground themselves."
