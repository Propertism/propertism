## 3. Internal Linking Strategy

### Rule 1 — Pillar ↔ Service Page (bidirectional)
Every pillar page must include a contextual CTA linking to its target service landing page. The service landing page must link back to the pillar in a "Learn More" or "NRI Guide" section.

### Rule 2 — Spoke → Pillar (always)
Every spoke article links to its cluster pillar in the first 200 words and once more in the conclusion.

### Rule 3 — Cross-cluster links (2–3 per article)
Each article links to 2–3 articles from adjacent clusters. Example: a POA article links to the Selling pillar and the Capital Gains spoke.

### Rule 4 — City context links
Articles about Chennai processes link to the corresponding city hub `/chennai/` and 1–2 city-intent pages.

### Rule 5 — No orphan articles
Every new article must appear in at least one sitemap-listed page before publishing.
