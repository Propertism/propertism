Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At Propertism, your privacy is our highest priority. This policy outlines how we collect, protect, and use your personal information.

1. Information Collection

We collect information when you register for NRI Assist, sign in via Google, or interact with our property concierge services. This includes name, email, and property preferences.

2. Data Usage

Your data is used strictly to provide personalized real estate guidance, coordinate with property care partners, and enhance your experience on our platform.

3. Security

All data transmitted through our platform is encrypted and stored in secure environments. We implement high-grade industrial security protocols to protect your portfolio information.

4. Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. Information is only shared with verified partners (e.g., legal counsel, property managers) with your explicit consent for service fulfillment.
