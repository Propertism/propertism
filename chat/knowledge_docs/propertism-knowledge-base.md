# Service Fee Structure

This document outlines the current fee structure for all services provided by Propertism Realty Advisors LLP. All fees are subject to the terms of the signed Service Agreement. Fees are exclusive of applicable GST unless stated otherwise.

## Property Management Fees

Property management covers tenant sourcing, lease management, rent collection, and periodic reporting.

**Annual Management Fee**: 8% to 10% of annual rental income, billed monthly.

- Standard properties (apartments, villas below ₹50,000 per month rent): 10% of monthly rent
- Premium properties (rent above ₹50,000 per month): 8% of monthly rent
- Minimum monthly fee: ₹3,000 regardless of rental income

The management fee covers:
- Monthly property inspection and condition report
- Rent collection and remittance to client account
- Tenant communication and issue resolution
- Quarterly financial statement

## Tenant Sourcing Fee

Tenant sourcing includes advertising, tenant screening, background verification, and lease drafting.

**One-time fee**: One month's rent (equivalent to the agreed monthly rental amount)

- Payable upon successful lease execution
- Covers up to three months of active sourcing
- Includes one complimentary lease renewal coordination

## Property Sale Advisory Fee

Advisory services for property sale including valuation, buyer sourcing, negotiation support, and transaction coordination.

**Sale Commission**: 1% to 2% of the final agreed sale price

- Properties below ₹1 Crore: 2% of sale value
- Properties between ₹1 Crore and ₹3 Crore: 1.5% of sale value
- Properties above ₹3 Crore: 1% of sale value (negotiable for premium mandates)

Minimum fee: ₹50,000 regardless of sale value.

## Maintenance Coordination Fees

Propertism coordinates maintenance on behalf of clients. Vendor costs are billed separately at actuals.

**Coordination Fee per Work Order**:
- Minor works (below ₹5,000 vendor cost): No coordination fee
- Standard works (₹5,000 to ₹50,000): 10% of vendor cost (minimum ₹500)
- Major works (above ₹50,000): 8% of vendor cost (minimum ₹4,000)

Emergency callouts outside business hours attract an additional ₹1,000 surcharge.

## Legal and Documentation Support

Support services for power of attorney, title verification, and documentation.

| Service | Fee |
|---------|-----|
| Power of Attorney guidance and coordination | ₹5,000 |
| Title verification report | ₹7,500 |
| Property tax support and filing | ₹3,000 per year |
| Encumbrance certificate assistance | ₹2,500 |
| NOC drafting and coordination | ₹3,500 |

## NRI Financial Remittance Support

Coordination of rental income remittance to NRI bank accounts under FEMA guidelines.

**Annual remittance support fee**: ₹6,000 per year

Covers:
- Monthly TDS calculation and deduction
- Form 15CA/15CB coordination with chartered accountant
- Annual FEMA compliance summary

Note: Chartered accountant fees for Form 15CB are billed separately at actuals (typically ₹2,500 to ₹5,000 per certificate).

## Payment and Billing

- All fees are billed in Indian Rupees (INR)
- GST at 18% is applicable on all service fees
- Invoices are issued at the beginning of each month for monthly fees
- One-time fees are invoiced upon service completion or milestone achievement
- Payment is due within 15 days of invoice date

## Fee Revision Policy

Propertism reviews its fee structure annually. Any revision will be communicated to active clients with 60 days advance notice. Existing service agreements will be honoured for their contracted term.
